The Tuner

Find out whose words your customer is repeating.

The situation

You are replaying a sales call with a security customer. The customer says 8 lines. Some are their own words, and some repeat a competitor's pitch. One competitor is already in this account.

Your goal

For each line, name who it came from and pick your reply. At the end, see which competitor is in the account.

What to do

  1. Read the customer's line on the white paper strip.
  2. Click a name under Source notes to read how that source talks.
  3. Click Next or Previous to tune the dial to that source.
  4. Click a reply in the Your reply list.
  5. Click Submit answer.

The Valve Room

Cut a customer's Splunk data. Keep everything that uses the data working.

The situation

Your goal

Round 1: find what fails under their plan. Round 2: change the plan to get to −30% or less, with nothing failing.

What to do

  1. Each row on the left is a data source. The amber plan tab shows their setting. The plate under the rows tells what each setting does.
  2. Click each name on the right. Read the data it needs. Then click Works or Fails.
  3. Click Run their plan.
  4. In round 2, click settings on the rows to change the plan. You can make six changes. Nothing is tested until you click Submit plan.
  5. Click Submit plan.

Works or fails?

This is the data it needs from each source. After their plan, does it still get this data?

The Valve Room

Cut a customer's Splunk data without breaking what depends on it.

The situation

A customer must index 30% less data each day. Their ops lead has a plan to do it. On the right are seven outputs: five alerts, a dashboard and a retention tank.

Your goal

Find what their plan breaks. Then make a plan that reaches −30% and breaks nothing.

What to do

  1. Their plan shows as dashed amber boxes on the rows at left.
  2. Click each output on the right. Mark it Will break or Will work.
  3. Press Run their plan.
  4. In round 2, click settings on the rows to change their plan. You get six changes.
  5. Press Submit plan.

What it needs

It breaks if any of these sources is set to something it can't use.
